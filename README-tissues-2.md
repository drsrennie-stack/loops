## The loop

- `id: "tissues-2"`, title **Tissues II Loop**, unit Tissues & Histology, module 1, system 2.
- 30 stations. Every station grades on two blanks, matching the Tissues loop.
  There are no self-marked stations.
- Frame numbering follows the house convention: station *k* uses `q = 2k`, `a = 2k+1`.
  Question frame and answer frame are the same picture; the answer frame adds the answer
  in bold under each prompt.

## What the stations cover

Twelve epithelial, thirteen connective including cartilage and bone, two muscle,
three nervous.

Six stations put an arrow on the picture instead of asking for the whole field:

| Station | Arrow points at | Asks for |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Ciliated apical border | Cilia, and what they do |
| 4 | Goblet cell in pseudostratified epithelium | Goblet cell, and what it does |
| 6 | Goblet cell in simple columnar epithelium | Goblet cell, and what it does |
| 7 | Apical brush border at the lumen | Microvilli, and what they do |
| 9 | Epidermis on the zoomed-in skin slide | Keratinized stratified squamous, and where |
| 13 | Dermis on the zoomed-out skin slide | Dense irregular CT, and what it does |

Two pictures are used more than once on purpose. The pseudostratified slide carries the
tissue ID, the cilia arrow and the goblet arrow. The simple columnar slide carries the
tissue ID, a goblet arrow and the brush border arrow. Students meet the same field asking
three different questions, which is the point.

## Cartilage, as settled in review

- Stations 18 and 19 are **both elastic cartilage**, two different panels of it, keyed to
  the external ear and epiglottis.
- Station 25 is **fibrocartilage**, keyed to intervertebral discs, menisci and the
  pubic symphysis.
- Station 30 is **hyaline cartilage**, from the slide sent separately on 25 August,
  keyed to the ends of long bones, trachea and costal cartilage.

Station 25 and station 30 currently sit outside the connective tissue run: 25 lands
between smooth muscle and the motor end plate, 30 sits at the end after nervous tissue.
Moving them up next to 18 and 19 renumbers every frame after that point, so it was left
alone. Say the word and it is one command.
